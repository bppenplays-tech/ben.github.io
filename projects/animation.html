About this work

Each short clip is a rendered Blender animation practising one or two of the 12 principles: squash and stretch and timing on the bouncing ball, overlapping action on the ball with a tail, ease in and out on the pendulum, and anticipation on the sword swing. I also put together a presentation on the 12 principles themselves.
